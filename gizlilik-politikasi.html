Mistwood Station – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

In short

Mistwood Station does not collect, store or share your personal information with anyone.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (chapters, items, settings, language) is kept only in your device’s local storage. It is never sent to us. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed with it.

What we do not collect

  • Personal information such as name, email, phone number, location or contacts
  • Advertising IDs, analytics or crash-reporting data (this version has none of these services)
  • Purchase information (this version has no in-app purchases and no ads)

Permissions

If vibration is enabled in settings, the app may use your device’s vibration. It requests no other special permissions.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds ads or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated beforehand.
